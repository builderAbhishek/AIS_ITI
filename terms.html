1. Nature of Website

This website serves solely as a public-facing informational and admission discovery platform for AIS Industrial Training Institute. It is designed to assist prospective candidates, parents, and visitors in understanding trade syllabi, eligibility requirements, workshop facilities, and career options.

2. Admission Inquiries & Seat Allocation

Submitting an online admission enquiry or application form through this website does NOT guarantee final admission or seat reservation. Final admission is subject to:

  • Meeting statutory minimum academic marks and age criteria
  • In-person physical verification of original marksheets, transfer certificate, and identity credentials
  • Availability of seats in the chosen trade under applicable state counseling quotas
  • Payment of statutory training fees as per institutional schedules

3. Ethical Disclaimer Regarding Job Guarantees

AIS ITI does not guarantee employment or specific salary packages. Vocational education equips students with practical craft competence, workshop discipline, and trade certification. Apprenticeship contracts (NAPS), industry employment, lateral diploma entry, and government examinations depend entirely on individual student diligence, merit, and employer/exam board evaluations.
