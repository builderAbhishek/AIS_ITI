Why CITS (Craft Instructor Training Scheme) Certification Matters

In India, CITS certification is conducted by National Skill Training Institutes (NSTIs) under the Directorate General of Training (DGT). It trains trade instructors in instructional methodology, workshop psychology, lesson planning, and standardized machine evaluation. Having CITS-trained mentors ensures that trainees receive standardized, accurate, and disciplined trade instruction.
