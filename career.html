Career Direction & Progression

Where Can ITI Take You?

An ITI qualification is not a dead-end; it is an open launchpad. Explore the realistic progression pathways available depending on aptitude, trade, and competitive performance.

Fast Transition

National Apprenticeship (NAPS)

Join registered industrial establishments as a paid technical apprentice. Receive a monthly stipend while earning the coveted National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) issued by NCVT.

  • Monthly government/company stipend
  • 1 to 2 years structured on-the-job training
  • Priority weightage in PSU technical recruitments
Assistance provided for NAPS portal registration.
Industry Sector

Private Sector Employment

Direct entry into private manufacturing plants, automobile dealerships, electrical contractors, fabrication yards, and construction infrastructure projects as junior technicians.

  • Machine Assembly & Alignment Fitter
  • Plant Electrical Maintenance Wireman
  • Dealership Automotive Diagnostic Technician
*Potential pathways based on candidate merit.
Higher Education

Polytechnic Diploma (Lateral Entry)

Holders of 2-year ITI certificates can enter directly into the 2nd Year (3rd Semester) of a 3-Year Diploma in Engineering under AICTE guidelines, saving one full academic year.

  • Lateral entry to Mechanical, Electrical, Civil Diploma
  • Qualification for Junior Engineer (JE) examinations
  • Pathway to B.Tech degree progression
State technical board rules apply.
Competitive Exams

Government & Defense Roles

Qualify for designated technician recruitments across Indian Railways (RRB Assistant Loco Pilot & Technician Grade-III), Metro Rail systems, State Electricity Boards, DRDO, and ISRO.

  • Railway RRB Technician / ALP exams
  • Electricity Board Lineman & Substation Staff
  • Defense Ordnance Factory & Dockyard trades
Selection via official government entrance exams.
Self-Reliance

Entrepreneurship & Contracting

Turn your vocational skills into a self-owned enterprise. Obtain state Wireman contractor licenses, start metal fabrication workshops, or launch automotive repair garages.

  • Licensed Electrical Contractor business
  • Welding, Grille & Gate Fabrication Unit
  • Multi-brand Two/Four Wheeler Service Station
Eligible for Mudra loan schemes.
Digital Services

Freelance CAD & Digital Centers

For COPA and Draughtsman Civil graduates: operate authorized Common Service Centers (CSC), offer freelance 2D CAD floor plans for local builders, or provide IT hardware support.

  • Architectural CAD Drafting & Estimation
  • CSC Digital Citizen Service Operator
  • Independent Computer & CCTV Installation Support
Flexible local enterprise models.
Responsible Transparency

Our Honest Policy on Placement & Careers

AIS ITI does NOT make false claims of "100% Guaranteed Jobs" or fabricated salary promises. Technical careers are earned through discipline, workshop attendance, craft mastery, and passing statutory trade examinations. Our role is to provide rigorous training, workshop machinery, and dedicated guidance for apprenticeship and competitive selections.
