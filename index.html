Skill-Based Education

Why Choose Practical ITI Training?

In an industrial economy, theoretical degrees alone often leave youth unprepared for work. ITI focuses directly on hands-on craft competence, tools, and real-world machinery.

70% Practical Workshop Learning

Spend the majority of your weekly curriculum inside machine shops, wiring booths, and computer labs instead of passive lectures.

Shorter Career Pathway

Complete your qualification in 1 to 2 years, enabling earlier entry into technical employment, apprenticeship stipends, or entrepreneurship.

National Apprenticeship (NAPS)

Direct qualification to apply for paid apprenticeship programs across Railways, PSUs, automotive plants, and heavy engineering firms.

Polytechnic Lateral Entry

2-Year ITI graduates can gain direct entry into the 2nd Year of a 3-Year Diploma in Engineering, opening pathways to Junior Engineer roles.

Self-Employment Confidence

Master the actual craft needed to start an electrical contracting business, auto service garage, welding fabrication shop, or IT service center.

Government Technical Exams

Eligible to compete for designated technician vacancies in Indian Railways (RRB ALP / Tech), State Electricity Boards, DRDO, and Metro Rail.

Systematic Pedagogy

The AIS ITI Training Model

Our structured 6-phase journey from basic workshop discipline to confident technical career readiness.

01

Learn Fundamentals

Engineering drawing, trade theory, electrical principles, and safety regulations.

02

Practice in Workshop

Bench filing, machine operations, panel wiring, engine disassembly, and diagnostics.

03

Build Projects

Design real working prototypes such as solar controllers, shearing jigs, and test fixtures.

04

Refine Skills

Tolerances, precision measurements, troubleshooting drills, and speed perfection.

05

Industry Exposure

Factory visits, guest masterclasses by industrial supervisors, and safety awareness.

06

Prepare for Career

National Apprenticeship registration, AITT trade exams, resume guidance, and mock interviews.

Where Can ITI Take You?

An ITI qualification provides multiple legitimate progression routes. We guide trainees toward the path aligned with their individual goals.

1

Apprenticeship & Industry Entry

Register on the NAPS portal, join a manufacturing or power facility as an apprentice, receive a government-backed stipend, and convert into an industrial technician.

ITI Training → NAPS Apprentice → Certified Technician
Learn more about NAPS
2

Higher Technical Education

Qualify for direct 2nd-year lateral entry to Polytechnic Diploma programs in Mechanical, Electrical, Civil or Automobile engineering, then proceed to B.Tech.

2-Yr ITI → 2nd Yr Diploma (Polytechnic) → Junior Engineer
View Lateral Entry rules
3

Self-Employment & Enterprise

Obtain licensed wireman certifications, establish welding fabrication centers, auto repair workshops, or plumbing contracting enterprises.

Practical Craft → Trade Licensing → Own Enterprise

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ITI eligibility, duration, practicals, and careers.

An Industrial Training Institute (ITI) is a dedicated post-secondary vocational training center under the DGT framework. Unlike purely academic degrees that focus on theory, ITI dedicates 70% of the syllabus to hands-on practical workshop execution—training students to operate real machinery, tools, and technical systems.
Eligibility varies by trade. Most engineering trades (Electrician, Fitter, Motor Mechanic, Electronics Mechanic) require 10th standard pass with Science and Mathematics. Trades such as Welder and Plumber are open to 8th or 10th pass candidates. COPA is open to 10th pass candidates from any stream.
1-Year trades (2 semesters): Welder, COPA, Plumber.
2-Year trades (4 semesters): Electrician, Fitter, Mechanic Motor Vehicle, Electronics Mechanic, Draughtsman Civil.
Yes. 2-Year ITI certificate holders are eligible for direct admission to the 2nd Year (Lateral Entry) of a 3-Year Polytechnic Diploma in relevant engineering branches under AICTE / State Technical Board rules. They can also enroll in CITS (Craft Instructor Training Scheme).
No genuine educational institution can guarantee employment. ITI gives you the practical technical skill and certification required to clear industry interviews, qualify for NAPS apprenticeships, take government technician tests (Railways, PSUs), or start your own technical contracting work.
