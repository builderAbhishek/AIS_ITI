Candidate Guidelines

Eligibility & Required Documents

Verify trade-wise educational criteria, minimum age guidelines, and prepare your original document folder for in-person counseling.

Regulatory Guidance: Requirements may vary by trade and state admission authority. Verify current state counseling notifications before submitting formal applications.

1. Educational Qualifications by Trade Category

Engineering Trades (2 Years)

10th Standard Passed

Candidates must have passed 10th standard (SSC / Matriculation) with Science and Mathematics as compulsory subjects from a recognized state/central education board.

Applicable Trades: Electrician, Fitter, Mechanic Motor Vehicle, Electronics Mechanic, Draughtsman Civil.
Fast-Track Trades (1 Year)

8th or 10th Standard Passed

Passed 8th standard or 10th standard depending on the state intake notification. Candidates with higher secondary qualifications can also apply.

Applicable Trades: Welder (Gas & Electric), Plumber.
Non-Engineering Digital

10th Passed (Any Stream)

Passed 10th standard examination in any stream (Arts, Commerce, or Science) from an accredited board. No prerequisite coding knowledge required.

Applicable Trades: Computer Operator & Programming Assistant (COPA).

2. Documents Checklist for Admission Verification

Academic Marksheets & Pass Certificates

Original and 3 self-attested photocopies of 10th standard marksheet (and 8th marksheet if applying for 8th-basis trades).

Identity & Residence Proof (Aadhaar)

Clear photocopy and original Aadhaar Card of candidate and parent/guardian for address and birth verification.

School Leaving / Transfer Certificate (TC)

Original School Leaving Certificate (SLC) or College Transfer Certificate (TC) countersigned as per state board rules.

Recent Passport-Size Photographs

6 identical recent passport-size color photographs with a clean white/light background.

Caste / Category Certificate (If Applicable)

Authorized SC / ST / OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) / EWS certificate issued by a competent revenue authority (Tehsildar/SDM).

Medical Fitness Certificate

Basic physical fitness certificate by a registered medical practitioner (MBBS) stating suitability for workshop labor.

3. Age & Physical Standards

Minimum Age: Candidate should generally have completed at least 14 years of age as of the 1st of August of the admission year. There is usually no upper age limit for vocational trade training under national frameworks.

Visual Acuity: Normal color vision is particularly recommended for candidates opting for the Electrician and Electronics Mechanic trades to distinguish standard wiring color codes.
