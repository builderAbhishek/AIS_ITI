General Information & Training Concept

An Industrial Training Institute (ITI) is a government-constituted post-secondary vocational training institute under the Directorate General of Training (DGT), Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. Its objective is to impart rapid, hands-on technical skills to youth—enabling them to enter the industrial workforce as skilled craftsmen, technicians, apprentices, or self-employed technical entrepreneurs.
In standard ITI programs, approximately 70% of weekly training hours are dedicated strictly to workshop practicals, bench work, machine operation, and circuit assembly. The remaining 30% covers Trade Theory, Engineering Drawing, Workshop Calculation & Science, and Employability Skills.

Admissions & Eligibility

Candidates who have passed 10th standard with Science and Mathematics can apply for all engineering trades (Electrician, Fitter, Motor Mechanic, Electronics Mechanic, Draughtsman). Candidates who have passed 8th or 10th standard can apply for Welder and Plumber. Candidates with any stream 10th pass can apply for COPA. The candidate should be at least 14 years old.
You will need original and 3 sets of self-attested photocopies of: 10th/8th Marksheet, Pass Certificate, School Leaving / Transfer Certificate (TC), Aadhaar Card, 6 Passport-size color photos, and Category/Caste Certificate (if claiming quota).
Duration is either 1 Year (e.g. Welder, COPA, Plumber) or 2 Years (e.g. Electrician, Fitter, Mechanic Motor Vehicle, Electronics Mechanic, Draughtsman Civil). Assessments follow the annual All India Trade Test (AITT) format.
