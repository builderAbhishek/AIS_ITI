Campus Culture

Student Life at AIS ITI

From hands-on prototype competitions to industrial study tours, campus athletics, and technical seminars—developing camaraderie, discipline, and pride in vocational craft.

Innovation

Student Capstone Projects

Final year trainees collaborate across trades to build functional prototypes, such as automated solar lighting rigs, mechanical punching jigs, and custom welding fixtures.

Showcased at regional skill fairs.
Industry Exposure

Factory & Substation Study Tours

Regular guided field visits to high-voltage electrical substations, automobile manufacturing facilities, and heavy machine shops to witness large-scale industrial operations.

Prepares trainees for plant floor realities.
Excellence

Skill Meets & Trade Olympiads

Internal and inter-institute timed competitions testing lathe turning precision, circuit wiring speed, and defect-free welding bead consistency.

Builds healthy competitive excellence.
Athletics

Annual Sports & Youth Tournaments

Inter-trade tournaments in volleyball, cricket, tug of war, and badminton that build stamina, discipline, and team spirit among trainees.

Active sports clubs and physical fitness.
Tradition

Vishwakarma Jayanti Celebration

Every year, trainees and instructors clean and worship workshop machinery, instruments, and tools in honor of Lord Vishwakarma, instilling deep reverence for craftsmanship.

Honoring the dignity of industrial labor.
Guest Lectures

Industry Masterclasses

Guest sessions delivered by factory maintenance heads, power grid supervisors, and dealership technical managers sharing current industry expectations.

Direct insights from working supervisors.
