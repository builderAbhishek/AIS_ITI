Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Session 2026–27

1. Introduction

AIS Industrial Training Institute ("AIS ITI", "we", "our", or "us") respects the privacy of prospective candidates, parents, students, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and protection of personal information submitted through our public website.

2. Information We Collect

When you fill out our admission enquiry form, schedule a campus visit, or apply for staff vacancies, we may collect:

  • Candidate's Full Name & Father/Guardian's Name
  • Mobile phone number (for WhatsApp or SMS counseling updates)
  • Email address
  • Educational qualification (8th, 10th, 12th pass status)
  • Trade interest of preference (Electrician, Fitter, Welder, COPA, etc.)

3. How We Use Your Information

Collected details are strictly used to:

  • Provide personalized admission counseling and verify trade eligibility
  • Send notifications regarding admission dates, counseling schedules, and fee structures
  • Schedule requested campus workshop tours
  • Process job applications for faculty and workshop positions

4. Data Sharing & Non-Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party telemarketers or external coaching agencies. Data is accessible solely to authorized admission desk personnel and faculty coordinators.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses minimal standard web cookies to preserve user navigation preferences and anonymous analytics to understand which trade curricula are most visited. No invasive tracking is employed.

6. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

If you wish to review, update, or request removal of your contact information from our counseling records, please email our admission desk at admissions@example.com or visit our campus office during working hours.
