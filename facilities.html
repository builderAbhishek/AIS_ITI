Campus Facilities

Designed specifically for vocational focus, technical discipline, safety, and student well-being.

Practical Bays

Trade-Specific Workshops

Individual bays for Electrical, Fitting, Welding, Motor Mechanics, and Electronics. Each bay features dedicated tool cribs and uninterrupted 3-phase industrial power.

Compliant with standard workshop area per trainee norms.
Digital Lab

Hi-Tech Computer & CAD Lab

Air-conditioned facility with 30 high-speed Intel Core-i5 workstations, high-speed fiber internet, and software suites for CAD drafting, database management, and CBT tests.

Online mock exam testing portal configured for trainees.
Resource Center

Technical Library & Reading Room

Extensive repository of Bharat Skills curricula, NIMI trade textbooks, engineering drawing reference manuals, and previous year All India Trade Test (AITT) question banks.

Quiet reading desks and digital catalog access.
Instruction

AV-Equipped Lecture Halls

Well-ventilated theory classrooms with digital projection setups for displaying 3D animated machinery cut-sections, engineering drawings, and safety demonstration videos.

Ergonomic student seating and natural ambient lighting.
Safety Center

Industrial Safety & First Aid

First-aid post with certified medical kit, emergency eyewash stations, CO2 and dry powder fire extinguishers at every bay, and mandatory PPE gear distribution.

Routine quarterly fire drills and safety audit logs.
Unbroken Power

3-Phase Power & Solar Generator

Heavy-duty industrial power distribution backed by silent on-site diesel generators and a grid-tied rooftop solar installation, ensuring continuous workshop running.

Zero practical downtime due to power interruptions.
Hygiene

RO Drinking Water & Washrooms

Multi-stage Reverse Osmosis (RO) chilled drinking water dispensers placed across campus floors, alongside separate, well-maintained sanitation facilities for boys and girls.

Daily hygiene audits and clean maintenance schedules.
Recreation

Sports Ground & Common Areas

Outdoor athletic grounds for volleyball, badminton, and annual youth sports meets, supplemented by a cafeteria and common break zones for student recreation.

Active sports teams competing at regional youth meets.
