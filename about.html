Our Heritage

Bridging the Gap Between Classroom Theory and Industrial Reality

Established in 2012, AIS Industrial Training Institute was conceived to provide youth from surrounding towns and districts with rigorous, affordable, and practical engineering trade skills.

Too often, post-10th education in our country focuses purely on rote textbooks, leaving graduates underprepared for actual shop-floor work. At AIS ITI, we constructed individual workshop bays equipped with center lathes, 3-phase test motors, inverter welding sets, and high-speed computer labs.

"We measure our success not by certificates issued, but by the practical confidence of a trainee holding a multimeter or operating a lathe machine."

Educational Framework

The 70 / 30 Practical Learning Philosophy

Industrial competence cannot be learned by sitting passively in front of blackboards. Our weekly training time allocation reflects the needs of modern industry:

70% Trade Practical Workshop bays, machinery, wiring booths
15% Trade Theory Principles, circuits, metallurgy, standards
15% Employability & Drawing Blueprints, digital tools, communication

How We Ensure Real Competency

Individual Machine Time: Trainees get designated turns on center lathes, drilling presses, and electrical test benches instead of merely crowding around a demonstrator.
CITS-Certified Instructors: Our teaching staff have completed Central Craft Instructor Training (NSTI / DGT), ensuring pedagogical mastery and industrial accuracy.
Live Defect Troubleshooting: Rather than just wiring static boards, instructors introduce real faults (short circuits, open loops, blown diodes) for trainees to locate using diagnostic instruments.
