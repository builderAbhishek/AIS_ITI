Workshops & Labs

Where technical blueprints turn into physical precision. Explore our dedicated, trade-specific practical training bays equipped with modern machines, safety systems, and individual tool stations.

Electrician Trade

Electrical Machine & Wiring Workshop

Dedicated practical bay equipped with industrial wiring test booths, AC/DC machine test benches, transformer testing sets, and earth electrode installation simulators. Trainees practice wiring commercial distribution boards and repairing motor windings.

Key Equipment: Motor-Generator Set, 3-Phase Induction Motors, Megger & Earth Resistance Testers, Panel Simulator.
Skills Learned: Distribution wiring, motor speed control, earth testing, troubleshooting panel breakers.
Batch Capacity: 24 Trainees • Safety: Isolated Earth Breakers & Rubber Mats
Fitter Trade

Mechanical Fitting & Machine Bay

Precision workshop featuring 30 heavy-duty bench vices, granite surface plates, precision height gauges, geared drilling machines, and center lathes. Trainees master manual filing tolerances to 0.02 mm and machine precision cylindrical parts.

Key Equipment: Geared Center Lathes, Precision Bench Vices, Radial Drill Press, Vernier Height Gauges.
Skills Learned: Facing, turning, threading, scraping, bench assembly, hydraulic valve fitting.
Batch Capacity: 24 Trainees • Safety: Mandatory Eye Protection & Machine Guards
Welder Trade

Welding & Fabrication Workshop

Dedicated multi-position welding booths equipped with inverter-based Manual Metal Arc (MMAW), Tungsten Inert Gas (TIG), and Metal Inert Gas (MIG/MAG) machines, motorized oxy-acetylene cutting tables, and heavy air extraction hoods.

Key Equipment: Inverter Arc & TIG Sets, MIG/MAG Wire Feeder Stations, Oxy-Acetylene Torches, Dye Penetrant Testing Rig.
Skills Learned: Flat/vertical/overhead welding, stainless steel TIG joining, gas cutting, weld bead testing.
Batch Capacity: 20 Trainees • Safety: Auto-Darkening Helmets & Fume Extraction
COPA Trade

Computer & Programming Lab

Air-conditioned 30-workstation digital laboratory connected by Gigabit Ethernet LAN with high-speed fiber internet. Trainees practice operating system administration, database queries, spreadsheet automation, web authoring, and CBT test simulations.

Key Equipment: Intel Core-i5 Workstations, Gigabit Managed Switches, Dual Boot Linux/Win, Network Rack Simulator.
Skills Learned: PC assembly/troubleshooting, SQL database commands, spreadsheet data management, HTML/CSS.
Batch Capacity: 30 Trainees • Safety: Dedicated Online UPS & Surge Suppressors
Motor Mechanic Trade

Automotive Engine & Diagnostics Bay

Spacious automobile service area fitted with a 2-post hydraulic vehicle hoist, live cut-away 4-stroke petrol and CRDi diesel test engines, computerized OBD-II multi-brand diagnostic scanner, and hydraulic brake bleeding stations.

Key Equipment: 2-Post Vehicle Lift, CRDi Diesel Engine Rig, OBD-II Computerized Scanner, Fuel Injector Calibrator.
Skills Learned: Engine overhaul, clutch & gearbox servicing, ECU fault code analysis, brake hydraulics.
Batch Capacity: 20 Trainees • Safety: Exhaust Gas Vent & Oil Containment Trays
Electronics Mechanic

Electronics & Microcontroller Lab

Electrostatic discharge (ESD) protected workshop equipped with dual-channel Digital Storage Oscilloscopes (DSOs), temperature-regulated SMD rework stations, microcontroller development boards, and audio/video display diagnostic test rigs.

Key Equipment: Digital Storage Oscilloscopes (DSO), SMD Hot-Air Rework, Function Generators, Arduino/8051 Kits.
Skills Learned: SMD component soldering, waveform tracing, microcontroller programming, SMPS power repair.
Batch Capacity: 20 Trainees • Safety: Anti-Static ESD Wristbands & Mats
